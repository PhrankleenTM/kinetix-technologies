The real cost of a slow website

By the Kinetix Team · 5 min read

Speed is not a technical detail. It is a business metric. A slow website costs you money every single day, here is how to measure it and fix it.

The money math

Every second of loading time eats into conversion. Delays mid-flow cost even more: a slow checkout loses sales at the exact moment a customer has decided to buy.

Hard to feel in a single visit. Put it on a statement for a quarter and the pattern is obvious: faster sites convert, rank, and retain.

The search engine penalty

Core Web Vitals are part of how search engines rank pages. A slow page ranks lower and earns fewer impressions, fewer visits, compounding month after month.

The brand cost

People make fast judgments about a business from the first click. A slow site quietly tells visitors your products are slow, your team is slow, your support will be slow.

Your website is your fastest employee. Speed it up.

How to measure it

Do not guess. Use real-user data, not just a lab test: largest contentful paint, interaction-to-next-paint, and performance on a mid-range phone on 4G.

  • Target under 2.5 seconds on mobile
  • Watch conversion rate on your slowest pages
  • Test every month, not once at launch

Practical fixes

Start with the cheap wins: compress images, remove render-blocking scripts, serve modern formats, and never ship code you are not using. Then do the harder work, lean templates, code-splitting, and sensible caching.

Speed is not a one-time optimization. Treat it like maintenance: keep it up and the business keeps its edge.

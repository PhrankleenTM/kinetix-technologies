Apex Corporate Website

Clean corporate website for a professional services firm.

Overview

Apex is a professional services firm whose website had fallen years behind their actual work. Prospects opened it, found it dated, and left.

We delivered a minimalist, fast site that puts clarity first, designed to convert a busy visitor into a qualified inquiry, not just a pageview.

The brief was deliberately strict: fewer words, fewer pages, fewer distractions. A firm that sells judgement and precision needed to look like it. Every screen was cut until only what helped a prospect decide remained.

The challenge

The old site had grown by addition, not by design. New services, new people, new pages, each appended on top of the last, until the message was buried under its own structure.

Three problems showed up in every conversation with the Apex team: the site was slow, it was hard to update, and visitors could not tell quickly what the firm actually did. Fixing any one of those without fixing all three would have left the website as the weak link in an otherwise strong business.

What we built

A minimalist, fast website with an information architecture organised around the firm’s three practice areas, not around their org chart.

  • Clarity-first information architecture
  • Fast, accessible, responsive pages
  • Conversion paths built around real services

The approach

We started by mapping every way a prospect could arrive and what they needed to learn at each step. That map became the skeleton of the site, and nothing was added unless it served a step on that journey.

From there, the build was deliberately boring: plain, robust code, a design system the Apex team could maintain themselves, and performance budgets checked on every page before it went live. The result is a site that stays fast and stays true to the practice as it grows.

The results

3x increase in qualified inquiries
  • Page load times cut below 1 second
  • Bounce rate on key pages dropped sharply

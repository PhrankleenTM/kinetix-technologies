E-Commerce Platform 2026

Cobalt Commerce Platform

A full e-commerce platform with payments, subscriptions, and order management for a B2B brand.

Overview

Cobalt built its business on long email threads and phone orders. Every order took hours to confirm, and easy reorders from happy customers quietly disappeared.

We shipped a complete e-commerce platform: a self-service catalog, secure payments, subscriptions for recurring purchases, and an order dashboard the team checks in seconds.

The shift was cultural as much as technical. Cobalt’s best customers happily reordered the same items every month, but asking them to re-type an order into an email was asking most of them to give up. A self-service store removed every step between “I need it again” and “it’s on its way”.

The challenge

Wholesale orders lived in email threads and phone calls.

Each order meant a back-and-forth for pricing, stock, and delivery dates, then a manual copy into the accounts system. The business lost money twice over: staff time on every order, and repeat customers who went elsewhere rather than chase a two-day email chain.

What we built

A self-service online store with payments, subscriptions, and order tracking, wired directly into the team’s daily view.

  • Self-service catalog with live stock & pricing
  • Payments, subscriptions & invoicing
  • Order dashboard with instant status updates

The approach

We mapped the handoff points first: where an order crossed from the customer to the salesperson to the warehouse to finance. Every one of those handoffs was a place where the old process leaked hours, so every one had to disappear into the new platform.

The catalog launch was phased to protect the existing sales rhythm. Existing customers were onboarded in small groups with personal setup, so the team never faced a sudden flood of unfamiliar requests. Within two months the email-order era was simply over.

The results

142% increase in online revenue
  • Orders handled without a single manual step
  • Repeat orders turned into one-click renewals

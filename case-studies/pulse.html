CRM & Business Tools 2026

CRM Suite

A sales CRM that unifies leads, follow-ups, and reporting for a growing services team.

Overview

The sales team was losing opportunities to the chaos of personal inboxes, sticky notes, and spreadsheets nobody updated.

We built a lightweight CRM where every contact, deal, and follow-up lives in one shared pipeline, clear enough that the team actually uses it every day.

The failure had never been effort. The team worked hard; they just worked apart. One account lived in a rep’s inbox, its history in that rep’s head, and its next step nowhere at all. When someone was out sick, the deal went dark with them.

The challenge

Leads scattered across inboxes and spreadsheets.

With no shared place to look, follow-ups depended on memory and good intentions. Warm leads went cold in a week, incoming enquiries were answered at whoever’s convenience, and reporting meant a painful half-day of spreadsheet archaeology each month.

What we built

A shared pipeline that tracks every contact, deal, and follow-up, designed to spend ten seconds a day, not ten minutes.

  • Unified contact & lead database
  • Deal pipeline with clear stages
  • Automatic follow-up reminders & reports

The approach

Most CRMs die because they ask busy sellers to feed them religion: log every call, every email, every thought. We inverted that. The platform does the logging, from the email the salesperson already sent, and asks for judgement only where a human is genuinely better: how hot is this deal, what happens next.

Onboarding was deliberately gradual. The first week was just contacts; the second week added pipelines; follow-up automation landed third. Each step proved itself before the next arrived, so the tool earned its place instead of being imposed in one intimidating launch.

The results

3x faster follow-up on new leads
  • First response time cut from hours to minutes
  • Every deal visible in one shared pipeline
